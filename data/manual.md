# Sample Bank Customer Service Operations Manual

This is a fictional manual for a Nigerian retail bank branch. All figures, fees, extensions and timelines are sample data. TAT means turnaround time. "Working days" are Monday to Friday excluding public holidays. Amounts are in Naira (₦).

## Individual Account Opening
Category: Accounts & KYC

Purpose: open a savings or current account for an individual customer.

Steps:
1. Confirm the customer is at least 18 years old and present in the branch.
2. Collect the completed account opening form and one passport photograph.
3. Verify the BVN (mandatory) and, where available, the NIN.
4. Sight the original valid ID: National ID, international passport, driver's licence or permanent voter's card.
5. Capture the customer's residential address and phone number.
6. Submit to the Customer Service Supervisor for approval.

Required documents: BVN, valid ID, passport photograph, proof of address (utility bill not older than 3 months) for Tier 3.

Timeline: Tier 1 and Tier 2 accounts are opened the same day. Tier 3 accounts are opened within 2 working days after address verification. Opening deposit: ₦1,000 for savings, ₦5,000 for current. Escalate failed BVN validation to the Branch Operations Manager.

## Corporate Account Opening
Category: Accounts & KYC

Purpose: open a current account for a registered company. Corporate accounts are opened in the branch only, with at least two directors or signatories present for the first visit.

Required documents:
- CAC certificate of incorporation (and CAC status report not older than 3 months)
- Memorandum and Articles of Association
- Board resolution authorising the account and naming the signatories
- Tax Identification Number (TIN)
- Utility bill for the company's business address (not older than 3 months)
- Valid ID and BVN of every director and signatory
- Passport photographs and specimen signatures of all signatories
- Completed account opening form and mandate card

Steps:
1. Collect and check every document against the checklist. Reject copies that are unclear or expired.
2. Verify the company on the CAC portal and validate each director's BVN.
3. Capture the specimen signatures and have the signatories sign the mandate card.
4. Submit the file to the Customer Service Supervisor for first approval.
5. The Supervisor forwards it to the Branch Operations Manager for final approval.
6. Open the account, issue the account number and set up the signing mandate.

Opening deposit: ₦50,000. Timeline: 5 working days from receipt of a complete file; address verification by a branch visit is part of this period. Incomplete files are not processed. Escalate companies with foreign directors, NGOs, and any director flagged as a politically exposed person to Compliance (ext. 4400) before approval. Escalate CAC verification failures to the Branch Operations Manager.

## KYC Tiers and Limits
Category: Accounts & KYC

Sample Bank uses three KYC tiers. Always confirm the customer's tier before approving limit requests.

Tier 1: requires name, phone number, date of birth and address. Maximum single deposit ₦50,000, maximum account balance ₦300,000.

Tier 2: requires BVN or NIN, a valid ID and a passport photograph. Maximum single deposit ₦100,000, maximum account balance ₦500,000. Daily transfer limit is ₦100,000.

Tier 3: requires everything in Tier 2 plus proof of address verified by a branch visit or utility bill. Daily transfer limit ₦5,000,000, with no maximum balance.

To upgrade a tier: collect the missing documents, submit the upgrade form, and the Customer Service Supervisor approves within 1 working day. Escalate upgrade requests above Tier 3 limits to Compliance (ext. 4400).

## BVN and NIN Issues
Category: Accounts & KYC

Purpose: resolve BVN or NIN mismatches that block account opening, upgrades or transactions.

Steps:
1. Ask the customer for their BVN and NIN and check the names against their ID.
2. If the name or date of birth on the BVN differs from the ID, the customer must correct the BVN record at the nearest BVN enrolment centre. The branch cannot edit BVN data.
3. If the phone number linked to the BVN is not the customer's, advise the customer to update it through the BVN enrolment centre.
4. If the NIN is not linked to the account, collect the NIN slip or NIN number and submit the NIN linking request.

Timeline: NIN linking is completed within 3 working days. BVN corrections are outside the bank's control and typically take 5 to 10 working days at the enrolment centre. Escalate unresolved BVN validation errors to E-Business (ext. 4300) after 2 failed attempts.

## Dormant Account Reactivation
Category: Accounts & KYC

An account becomes dormant after 12 months with no customer-initiated transaction. Bank charges and interest credits do not count as customer-initiated transactions.

Steps:
1. Confirm the customer's identity with a valid ID and BVN.
2. Collect the dormant account reactivation form and a recent utility bill (not older than 3 months).
3. Check the account for any restrictions or post-no-debit flags.
4. Submit to the Customer Service Supervisor for approval.

Timeline: reactivation is completed within 24 hours of a complete request. Accounts with balances above ₦1,000,000 require approval from the Branch Operations Manager. Accounts dormant for more than 5 years require Compliance (ext. 4400) approval and take up to 5 working days.

## Change of Phone Number or Email
Category: Accounts & KYC

Phone number and email changes can only be done in the branch, never by phone or chat.

Steps:
1. Verify the customer with a valid ID and BVN.
2. Collect the signed change of contact details form.
3. Confirm the new phone number by sending a test code to it.
4. If the BVN-linked phone number differs from the new number, advise the customer to update the BVN record first.
5. Update the record and have the Customer Service Supervisor authorise the change.

Timeline: the change takes effect within 24 hours. SMS alerts and OTPs move to the new number only after the change is authorised. For security, a 24-hour hold applies to new-device activation after a phone number change. Escalate suspected SIM-swap cases to the Fraud Desk (ext. 4100).

## Account Closure
Category: Accounts & KYC

Steps:
1. Verify the customer's identity with a valid ID and BVN.
2. Collect a signed written closure request stating the reason.
3. Confirm there are no outstanding loans, overdrafts, standing orders or pending disputes.
4. Collect all unused cheque leaves and cards.
5. Pay out the balance by transfer to another account or by bank draft. Cash payout above ₦500,000 needs Branch Operations Manager approval.
6. Submit for approval by the Customer Service Supervisor.

Timeline: closure is completed within 3 working days. A closure fee does not apply. Accounts with unresolved disputes cannot be closed until the dispute is resolved.

## Statement Requests
Category: Accounts & KYC

Customers can view and download statements on the mobile app for free.

Branch-issued statements:
1. Verify the customer with a valid ID.
2. Collect the statement request form stating the period needed.
3. Print and stamp the statement.

Fees and timelines: up to 6 months, same day, ₦1,000 per statement. 7 to 24 months, within 3 working days, ₦2,000. Statements older than 24 months are archived and take up to 7 working days, ₦3,000. Sending a statement to a third party requires the customer's written consent.

## Reference Letters and Account Confirmation
Category: Accounts & KYC

Customers request reference letters for visa applications, rent or tenders.

Steps:
1. Verify the customer's identity and confirm the account has been active for at least 6 months.
2. Collect the request form stating the purpose and addressee.
3. Prepare the letter on bank letterhead. The Customer Service Supervisor signs it.

Fee: ₦5,000 per letter. Timeline: 2 working days. Letters for embassy use take 3 working days. Escalate requests that ask for credit-worthiness statements to the Branch Operations Manager.

## Failed ATM Withdrawal (Account Debited, No Cash)
Category: Cards & ATM

Purpose: handle cases where the customer's account was debited but the ATM did not dispense cash.

Steps:
1. Ask for the card's last four digits, the ATM location, the date, time and amount.
2. Confirm the debit on the account statement.
3. Check whether an auto-reversal has already posted.
4. Log a complaint in the complaint system and give the customer the reference number.
5. Tell the customer the reversal timeline and follow up.

Timeline: on Sample Bank ATMs, reversal happens automatically within 24 hours. On another bank's ATM, reversal takes up to 5 working days. If no reversal after 5 working days, escalate to Card Operations (ext. 4200). Required: debit alert or statement, card last four digits, ATM location.

## Card Blocking
Category: Cards & ATM

Block a card immediately when the customer reports it lost, stolen or compromised.

Steps:
1. Verify the customer with a valid ID, BVN or registered phone number.
2. Block the card in the card management system. Blocking takes effect instantly.
3. Confirm with the customer that they received the block confirmation SMS.
4. Ask whether any unauthorised transactions occurred. If yes, start the Unauthorized Debit Dispute procedure and alert the Fraud Desk (ext. 4100).
5. Offer card replacement.

Customers can also block cards on the mobile app or by dialling the short code *5550#. A blocked card cannot be unblocked if it was reported stolen. Escalate if the block fails in the system to Card Operations (ext. 4200) at once.

## Card Replacement
Category: Cards & ATM

Replacement applies to lost, stolen, damaged, expired or blocked cards.

Steps:
1. Block the old card first if it is still active.
2. Collect the card request form and a valid ID.
3. Debit the replacement fee of ₦1,000 from the customer's account.
4. Submit the request to Card Operations.

Timeline: the new card is ready for pickup at the branch within 5 working days. The customer must come in person with a valid ID to collect and activate the card, and sets a PIN at the ATM or PIN pad. Cards not collected within 60 days are destroyed. Escalate delays beyond 7 working days to Card Operations (ext. 4200).

## ATM PIN Reset
Category: Cards & ATM

The card is locked after 3 wrong PIN attempts.

Steps:
1. Verify the customer with a valid ID and the card.
2. Confirm the card is not blocked or reported stolen.
3. Unlock the card in the card system and trigger a PIN reset.
4. The customer sets a new PIN on the branch PIN pad. Staff must never see, write down or ask for a PIN.

Timeline: reset is immediate at the branch. The new PIN works on all ATMs and POS terminals within 30 minutes. PIN reset is free. If the customer has forgotten the PIN and does not have the card, block the card and issue a replacement.

## Card Swallowed by ATM
Category: Cards & ATM

Steps:
1. Ask for the ATM location, date and time.
2. Block the card immediately as a precaution.
3. Log a request with Card Operations to retrieve the card if the ATM belongs to Sample Bank.
4. Advise the customer to request a replacement card.

Timeline: cards swallowed at Sample Bank ATMs are retrieved within 2 working days. A retrieved card can be collected after the customer shows valid ID. Cards swallowed at other banks' ATMs are not recoverable by Sample Bank. Replacement follows the Card Replacement procedure.

## POS Payment Failed but Account Debited
Category: Cards & ATM

Steps:
1. Ask for the merchant name, date, time, amount and card last four digits.
2. Confirm the debit on the statement and check for an auto-reversal.
3. Log a complaint and give the customer the reference number.

Timeline: automatic reversal happens within 24 hours for same-bank POS transactions and up to 5 working days for other banks' POS terminals. If no reversal after 5 working days, escalate to Card Operations (ext. 4200). Required: debit alert or statement and the merchant's receipt if available.

## Failed Transfer (Debited, Beneficiary Not Credited)
Category: Transfers & Payments

Steps:
1. Ask for the transfer date, amount, beneficiary account number, beneficiary bank and session ID or reference.
2. Confirm the debit on the statement and check whether an auto-reversal has posted.
3. Check the transfer status in the transfer system: successful, pending or failed.
4. If failed, ensure the reversal posts. If pending, wait 24 hours.
5. Log a complaint and give the customer the reference number.

Timeline: failed transfers are auto-reversed within 24 hours. If not reversed after 24 hours, resolution takes up to 3 working days after logging. If still unresolved after 5 working days, escalate to E-Business (ext. 4300). If the transfer succeeded to the wrong account, use the Wrong Beneficiary Transfer procedure.

## Double Debit
Category: Transfers & Payments

A double debit means the same transaction was debited twice from the customer's account.

Steps:
1. Ask for both debit dates, amounts and the transaction reference.
2. Confirm on the statement that two debits with identical amounts and references exist.
3. Check with the merchant or beneficiary that only one was credited.
4. Log a complaint and give the customer the reference number.

Timeline: investigation takes 2 working days. Reversal of a confirmed double debit is posted within 3 working days of logging. Required: statement showing both debits. Escalate to E-Business (ext. 4300) if the reversal is not posted within 5 working days.

## Wrong Beneficiary Transfer
Category: Transfers & Payments

Sample Bank cannot reverse a successful transfer without the recipient's consent.

Steps:
1. Collect the transfer details, the amount, the account number sent to and the beneficiary bank.
2. Ask the customer to complete a recall request form and sign it.
3. Submit the recall request to the beneficiary bank through E-Business.
4. Inform the customer that the recipient must agree before funds are returned.

Timeline: the recall request is sent within 24 hours. The beneficiary bank typically responds within 5 working days. Escalate to E-Business (ext. 4300) if no response after 7 working days. Never promise the customer a refund.

## Complaint Handling and Resolution Timelines
Category: Disputes & Complaints

All complaints must be logged in the complaint system, even if resolved on the spot.

Steps:
1. Listen to the customer and record the complaint with date, account, amount and details.
2. Give the customer a reference number.
3. Acknowledge the complaint within 24 hours in writing (SMS or email).
4. Investigate and resolve.
5. Inform the customer of the outcome in writing.

Timelines: simple complaints (failed ATM, POS, transfer, double debit) are resolved within 5 working days. Complex complaints are resolved within 15 working days, with a written update every 5 working days. If the customer is unhappy with the outcome or the 15-working-day limit has passed, advise them they may escalate to the Central Bank of Nigeria Consumer Protection Department. Escalate any complaint older than 10 working days to the Head, Customer Experience (ext. 4500).

## Unauthorized Debit Dispute
Category: Disputes & Complaints

Purpose: investigate a debit the customer says they did not authorise.

Steps:
1. Verify the customer's identity.
2. Block the card or restrict the account if the customer believes it is compromised.
3. Collect the dispute form with the transaction date, amount, merchant or channel and the customer's statement of what happened.
4. Attach the account statement, debit alert and ID.
5. Submit the dispute to the Fraud Desk (ext. 4100) and log a complaint.

Timelines: the customer must report the debit within 60 days of the transaction. Investigation takes 10 working days. International and card-not-present disputes may take up to 15 working days. Outcome is communicated in writing. Provisional credit is not given. Escalate to the Fraud Desk (ext. 4100) immediately for amounts above ₦500,000.

## Mobile App Login Problems
Category: Digital Banking

Steps:
1. Confirm the customer has the latest app version from the official app store.
2. If the password is forgotten, guide the customer to use "Forgot password" and reset with OTP sent to the registered phone number.
3. If the app says "device not recognised", the customer must re-register the device using card details and OTP.
4. If the profile is locked after 5 wrong attempts, unlock it at the branch after verifying ID, or via the call centre after security questions.
5. If the registered phone number is old, follow the Change of Phone Number or Email procedure first.

Timeline: profile unlock is immediate after verification. New-device activation is delayed by 24 hours after a phone number change. Escalate persistent errors, with a screenshot and the device model, to E-Business (ext. 4300).

## Token and OTP Issues
Category: Digital Banking

OTP not received:
1. Confirm the phone number on the account matches the customer's current number.
2. Check that the phone has network and the SMS inbox is not full.
3. Ask the customer to wait 2 minutes, then request a new OTP. OTPs expire after 5 minutes.
4. Check for a DND (do-not-disturb) block on the line. The customer must disable it with the network provider.

Hardware token problems: if the token is lost, damaged or not generating codes, block it and issue a replacement at ₦2,500. Replacement tokens take 3 working days. Escalate OTP delivery failures lasting more than 24 hours to E-Business (ext. 4300). Never read out or request an OTP from a customer.

## Suspected Fraud
Category: Security & Fraud

Treat every suspected fraud case as urgent.

Steps:
1. Stay calm and verify the caller or customer.
2. Block the card and restrict internet and mobile banking access immediately.
3. Record the details: transactions, dates, amounts, how the customer was contacted.
4. Call the Fraud Desk (ext. 4100) within 1 hour of the report and send the case summary by email.
5. Log a complaint and give the customer the reference number.
6. Advise the customer to change their passwords and not to share OTPs, PINs or card details with anyone.

Timeline: the Fraud Desk contacts the customer within 4 hours and completes the initial review within 3 working days. Funds that are still recoverable are placed on hold by the Fraud Desk. For amounts above ₦500,000, also notify the Branch Operations Manager at once. Customers may also report to the police and obtain a report number.

## Phishing and Shared Credentials
Category: Security & Fraud

Use this procedure when a customer shared their PIN, OTP, password or card details after a suspicious call, SMS or link.

Steps:
1. Block the card and lock mobile and internet banking immediately.
2. Review the last 48 hours of transactions with the customer.
3. Escalate to the Fraud Desk (ext. 4100) within 1 hour.
4. After the Fraud Desk clears the account, reset the password and issue a replacement card.

Reminder: Sample Bank staff never ask customers for a PIN or OTP. Losses caused by customers voluntarily sharing credentials are reviewed case by case and are not refunded automatically.

## Escalation Matrix
Category: Escalation

Level 1: Customer Service Officer. Handles the request and logs the complaint.
Level 2: Customer Service Supervisor. Take the case if unresolved after 30 minutes or if the customer asks for a supervisor.
Level 3: Branch Operations Manager. Take the case if unresolved after 2 hours, or for amounts above ₦500,000, or for approvals marked in this manual.
Level 4: Head, Customer Experience (ext. 4500). Take the case if a complaint is older than 10 working days or the customer threatens legal action.

Specialist desks: Fraud Desk ext. 4100 (fraud, unauthorised debits, SIM swap). Card Operations ext. 4200 (cards, ATM and POS reversals). E-Business ext. 4300 (transfers, mobile app, OTP, token). Compliance ext. 4400 (KYC, BVN, dormant accounts over 5 years).

Always give the customer the complaint reference number when escalating, and tell them who will contact them and by when.

## After-Hours and Emergency Escalation
Category: Escalation

The branch closes at 4:00 PM on working days. Fraud and card blocking requests received after hours go to the 24-hour contact centre.

Steps:
1. Direct the customer to dial *5550# or use the mobile app to block the card immediately.
2. Log the case in the complaint system with the time of the report.
3. The contact centre passes urgent fraud cases to the on-call Fraud Desk officer (ext. 4100).
4. The branch follows up on the next working day by 10:00 AM.

Non-urgent complaints received after hours are logged and acknowledged within 24 hours.
